IndexMyPlex — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

IndexMyPlex is a Chrome browser extension that exports your local Plex media library to common document formats. This policy explains what data the extension accesses and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing. IndexMyPlex does not transmit any data to any external server.

Data Stored Locally

The extension stores two items on your device using Chrome's local storage:

  • Your Plex server URL (e.g. http://localhost:32400)
  • Your Plex authentication token

These are stored solely to avoid requiring you to re-enter them each time you open the extension. They never leave your device.

Plex Authentication Token

When you click Connect, the extension reads your Plex authentication token from your open Plex browser tab. This token is used only to communicate with your local Plex server. It is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any third party.

Your Media Data

The extension reads your Plex library metadata (titles, directors, cast, genres) to generate export files. All processing happens locally in your browser. The exported files are saved directly to your device. No media data is sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

IndexMyPlex does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services.
